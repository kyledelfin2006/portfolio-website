## The problem

A coffee shop runs on two connected jobs: ringing up orders at the counter and keeping track of what is in stock. Kofi brings both into one Java application, a point-of-sale and inventory management system made for coffee shops.

## What Kofi covers

Kofi combines point-of-sale and inventory management. The point-of-sale side handles sales at the counter, and the inventory side tracks the stock a coffee shop depends on.

## Tools

Kofi is written in Java and built with Maven. SQLite is its database.
